THE PLASTIC CRISIS

Why does plastic pollution matter?

Plastic pollution is not simply litter. Plastic waste can remain in the environment for hundreds of years and gradually break down into smaller particles.

01

Animal Entanglement

Sea turtles, dolphins and seabirds can become trapped in fishing nets, plastic rings and other debris.

02

Ingestion

Marine organisms may mistake plastic fragments for food, allowing plastic pollution to enter aquatic food webs.

03

Coral Reef Damage

Floating debris can cover coral reefs and seagrass, reducing access to sunlight and damaging important habitats.

04

Microplastics

UV radiation and environmental processes can break larger plastics into tiny fragments that are much harder to remove.

05

Economic Damage

Pollution can hurt coastal tourism, fisheries and boats while increasing the cost of cleaning polluted areas.

06

Food-Web Pollution

Tiny organisms can ingest plastic particles, allowing contaminants to move through different levels of the food chain.

AWARENESS

Plastic does not simply disappear.

The longer plastic remains in the environment, the more difficult it becomes to remove.

CLEAN OCEAN X ROBOTICS

Technology that fights pollution.

🚤

SOLUTION 01

Ocean-Skimmer Drones

Solar-powered aquatic drones use conveyor systems and smart intake filtration to collect surface plastics, marine debris and other pollution.

  • ☀️ Solar powered
  • 🌊 Surface cleaning
  • 🐢 Wildlife-conscious operation
🤖

SOLUTION 02

Beach-Crawler Drones

Autonomous tracked robots can comb beach sand and remove cigarette butts, bottle caps, micro-debris and other waste.

  • 🏖️ Beach cleaning
  • 🔎 Fine debris detection
  • ♻️ Waste collection

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

The robots can think, detect and adapt.

01

Computer Vision

AI analyzes camera feeds to classify different types of debris and help avoid marine wildlife.

02

Pollution Heatmaps

GPS telemetry can be used to create pollution maps and identify areas that require attention.

03

Remote Fleet Command

Operators can monitor robot fleets, battery levels, cleaning boundaries and automated returns.

04

Smart Notifications

Beach operators can receive notifications when collected waste reaches capacity.

DEPLOYMENT STRATEGY

From ports to beaches.

01

Port Hubs

Establish automated docking stations in major coastal locations such as Alexandria, Sharm El Sheikh and Hurghada.

02

Estuary Barriers

Intercept plastic entering the sea through river mouths before it reaches open water.

03

Public Beaches

Deploy crawler fleets along tourist coastlines to maintain cleaner beaches.

04

Circular Recycling

Sort and wash collected plastic before sending it to recycling partners for potential reuse.
